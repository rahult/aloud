Your clipboard,
read aloud.

Chirp lives in your menu bar and turns any text you copy into natural speech — with a neural model that runs entirely on your machine. Nothing you copy ever leaves it.

Download for macOS then press ⌃⇧Space anywhere
1

Copy anything

An article, a diff, an email thread, a page of docs. If you can select it, Chirp can read it.

2

Press the shortcut

Ctrl/⌘+Shift+Space from any app. Press it again to stop.

3

Keep working

Eleven voices, instant playback, zero network calls. The Kokoro-82M model runs on your CPU.

Deliberately small

  • Fully on-device — no API keys, no accounts, no audio in the cloud.
  • A menu-bar resident, not another window to manage.
  • An HTTP API on 127.0.0.1:8789 so your own tools can speak too.
